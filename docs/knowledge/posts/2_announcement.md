I have been working on a fun side project that pulls your games from Chess.com and runs analysis on them. It is aimed at people who enjoy stats and data visualisations, and want to understand their chess games a bit more.



Some of the things it can show you:

- How often you win, lose, or draw, and how that changes over time

- Whether you tend to do better in shorter games or longer ones

- How your results vary by opening, playing colour, and time control

- A win-probability estimator that gives a sense of how rating differences and colour influence outcomes.



The app is live here:

https://chess-analysis.streamlit.app



It would be great to hear what you think, or what kinds of analysis you would like to see added next.
